Is this just a Granola MCP?

No, Roy is the organisational memory harness that pulls info from any source, organises it for what you care most about and answers through whatever AI tool your team already uses.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere. Nothing leaves your workspace, and every answer Roy gives comes with the source it came from.

What if Roy gets something wrong?

He doesn't guess. If new information conflicts with what's already on record, Roy flags the conflict instead of picking a side.
